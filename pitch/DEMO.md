# Sehat · 60-second backup demo (GRAVITON 2026 · Team ANTIJAREX)

Use this if Wi-Fi is weak or time is short. Everything runs at **https://sehat-ashen.vercel.app**.

**Before you start:** open **/hub/demo.html** → press **Reset** → check the chip says **● Live network · room GRAVITON**.

| Time | Do | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0–8 s | Show the home page | "India has about one allopathic doctor per 1,200 people, and up to two in three chronic patients don't take medicines as prescribed. Sehat connects everyone in that chain." |
| 8–15 s | Open **Live demo**, press **▶ Auto-play** | "Five roles, five devices, one live network. Dr. Saathi walks through it." |
| 15–25 s | **Patient** books (fever) → **City Care** sees it, checks in | "Rahul books from his symptoms. Sehat picks the right doctor and slot, and City Care sees it instantly." |
| 25–35 s | **Doctor** signs → **Pharmacy** gets it with a stock check | "Dr. Bansal signs. The prescription goes to Rahul's chosen pharmacy, with live stock." |
| 35–42 s | Pharmacy sends the **bill**, Rahul **approves**, rider assigned | "Nothing is packed until the patient approves the bill. Then a rider takes it to his door." |
| 42–50 s | **Supply chain**: AI insight → PO → **Supplier** delivers, stock goes up | "When shelves run low, the AI drafts the order, the distributor delivers, and stock updates itself." |
| 50–60 s | Patient → **Records & OCR** (prescription photo, Punjabi) or **Senior Mode** | "Any report photo is explained in ten Indian languages, and Senior Mode gives elders three big buttons with spoken reminders and SOS. Your journal stays private until you share it." |

**If something breaks:** Auto-play works without AI keys. If the network drops, every screen keeps working on that device (the chip says "This device only").

**If asked "is this new?":** "Sehat Copilot, the patient app, was our earlier project. At GRAVITON we built the network around it: hospital, doctor, nurse, pharmacy and supplier roles, live routing across devices, the supply chain, Senior Mode and consent-based privacy."

**If asked about privacy:** "Each screen shows only what that role needs; journals reach the doctor only with consent. Database-level access control per user is our next step."
